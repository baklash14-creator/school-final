FAQ

Common questions

How do I apply?

Use the application form on this page. You will receive a reference code that you can use to check the status of your application.

How do parents see their child's results?

Once the school creates your parent account, sign in to the portal. Results, attendance and fees for each child are listed there.

When do I get my portal login?

Accounts are created by the school after enrolment. You will be given a temporary password and can change it after signing in.

Can I pay fees online?

The portal shows balances and receipts. Payments are recorded by the school office; ask the office for the payment methods it accepts.
